Private Marketplace

Curate private catalogs of approved products in AWS Marketplace

Private Marketplaces are unique sets of vetted software available in AWS Marketplace for AWS organizational units or different AWS accounts within their organization to purchase.

Create multiple private marketplace catalogs, manage catalog contents, and more.

About

Introduction to Private Marketplace (1:31)

In a Private Marketplace, users can easily find and deploy vetted products that comply with your organization's internal policies, enabling your teams to move fast while maintaining purchasing controls.

Administrators can customize their Private Marketplace with a company logo, color scheme, and messaging so their users know they are in an approved environment.

How it works

Create experience
Administrators create a Private Marketplace experience to govern a set of accounts.
Curate products
Administrators create a list of approved products vetted for their user's use in AWS Marketplace.
Customize branding
Administrators update catalog title, descriptions, logo, and color scheme that suit their experience.
Associate OUs / accounts
Administrators select AWS Organizational Units (OUs) or accounts in the organization to be governed by an experience.
Manage experience
Administrators review software requests from users, and add additional products, modify settings and branding.

Benefits and features

Gain control

Utilize a Private Marketplace to limit AWS Organizational Units and corresponding accounts to only subscribe to approved products.

Multiple catalogs

Create multiple Private Marketplace catalogs to have a different curated catalog target a different group of accounts, organized by your AWS Organizations structure.

Streamline approvals

A user governed by Private Marketplace can request products be added to their Private Marketplace by sending it to an admin to streamline the approval process.

Branding settings

Customize a Private Marketplace's experience with a different look and feel with their organization's name, description, logo, and color scheme.

Delegation and ownership

A management administrator can register an account in the organization as delegated administrator to manage Private Marketplace on its behalf while maintaining the ownership of Private Marketplace resources.

Hierarchical governance

Utilize inherited governance rules through AWS Organizations to automatically send out updates to different levels of accounts in a Private Marketplace.

Users

Users within Private Marketplace are defined by the policies of AWS Identity and Access Management.

Administrators

Private Marketplace administrators curate catalogs of approved third-party products within AWS Marketplace. They also vet software requests from users and modify settings and branding. A management account administrator can delegate administration access to a trusted AWS account within an organization.

Users

Private Marketplace users are subscribers of the approved third-party products in AWS Marketplace. Users may also have the ability to request an administrator to add additional products to their Private Marketplace catalog.

Use cases

Enterprise customers

For organizations that have software procurement policies and processes in place, Private Marketplace provides controls to ensure users are operating within those policies while using AWS Marketplace. Multiple Private Marketplace catalogs can be curated for the organization's different sets of users and their software needs, helping users easily find, buy, and deploy products approved for their use.

Channel partners

Private Marketplace enables channel partners to curate a catalog of approved products for managed customers accounts. Private Marketplace can be customized to adhere to the customer's procurement policies, including managing of the administrative permissions and updating the look and feel to align with branding.
